Motor control components & basic circuits

1 · Power circuit (3-phase starter)

This is the high-current path to the motor. The contactor’s main contacts (M) switch it; the control circuit below decides when.

Dwg 1 · Power circuitFull-voltage, non-reversing

How to read it

  • L1–L3 are the incoming phases (the 85-MT5 bench is fed 208 VAC, 3-phase).
  • The dashed line means the three disconnect blades move together.
  • All three M contacts close when the M coil (in the control circuit) is energized.
  • Each OL heater carries motor current. If it overheats, the OL contact in the control circuit opens.
  • Swapping any two leads (e.g. T1 and T3) reverses the motor. A reversing starter does that with a second contactor.

2 · Control circuit: 3-wire start/stop with seal-in

This is the core circuit of the course, and most other circuits build on it. It runs on the control transformer’s X1–X2 output.

Dwg 2 · 3-wire controlWire nos. 1-2-3

Sequence of operation

  • Press START: current flows 1→2→3 through the M coil. M pulls in and the motor runs.
  • The M auxiliary contact (rung 2) closes too and bypasses START. This is the seal-in (holding) contact, so the motor keeps running after you release START.
  • Press STOP or let the OL trip: the coil drops out, the seal-in opens, and the motor stays off until START is pressed again.
  • Wiring order that matches the drawing: X1 → Stop → Start → coil → OL → X2. Keep the coil next to X2.

3 · Reversing control with interlocks

The trainer has two reversing contactors, F (forward) and R (reverse). Each coil has a seal-in, and an N.C. contact from the opposite contactor keeps both from pulling in together.

Dwg 3 · Reversing controlElectrical + mechanical interlock

What to notice

  • Electrical interlock: the N.C. R contact sits in the F rung and the N.C. F contact sits in the R rung. With F running, the R coil can’t energize.
  • Mechanical interlock (dashed) is a physical bar between the two contactors that does the same job.
  • One set of OL contacts protects both directions.
  • In the power circuit, R’s main contacts are wired with two phases swapped (L1↔L3), which reverses rotation.
  • To change direction you press STOP first. If you add N.C. pushbutton interlocks, you can switch directly.

4 · On-delay timer

This uses the multi-function timer set to on-delay. Energize TR, and its timed contact closes after the preset time.

Dwg 4 · Timer controlOn-delay (NOTC)

Variations you’ll wire

  • On-delay (NOTC): the contact closes after the coil has been on for the preset time.
  • Off-delay (NOTO): the contact opens after the coil turns off. Good for cooling-fan run-on.
  • Swap the pilot light for a second contactor coil to build sequence starting (motor 2 starts X seconds after motor 1) or a timed stop.
