Before using this in lab

This redraw follows the supplied picture, not a photograph of your actual setup. Instructor verification is still needed for supply voltage, transformer links, motor nameplate and protective grounding. Keep power isolated when making connections. X2 (secondary 5) is the control return; do not substitute incoming supply neutral.

The unused stations are simplified. The connected stations keep their physical positions and terminal order. The reference’s transformer earth bond is shown; other required equipment earth bonds are not detailed.
